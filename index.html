Our Heritage

Over Four Decades Along the Susquehanna

From a single production in a rural community hall in 1980 to our permanent downtown Main Street playhouse, Winding River Players has been built entirely by neighbors who love live theatre.

1980 • The Curtain Rises

“Our Town” at Ulster Community Hall

Founders Steve Weiss, Jenny Berg, and Dotti Costello established Winding River Players with a debut staging of Thornton Wilder’s classic Our Town at Ulster Community Hall—sparking a lasting theatrical tradition in Bradford County.

’80
’90s
The Touring & Dinner-Theater Era

Community Venues & Barclay School Benefits

Led by dedicated local theatre champions Julie Larnard-Newbury, John Thomas, and Joe Newbury, the troupe staged beloved dinner-theater productions at the Elks Club, Towanda Country Club, and Sons of Italy—including cherished annual benefit shows for the Barclay School.

2001 & 2021 • A Permanent Stage

415 Main Street

In 2001, Winding River Players opened a permanent home in downtown Towanda at 415 Main Street—The Black Box Theatre. In 2021, the stage was officially dedicated and renamed the Joe Newbury Theatre in honor of Joe Newbury’s decades of artistic leadership.

’01
Now
Today • Regional Arts Hub

Serving Bradford & Sullivan Counties

With a newly refreshed historic storefront on Main Street and partnerships spanning the Keystone Theatre, local schools, and downtown businesses, Winding River Players continues to mentor youth performers and welcome audiences of all ages.
