Take Your Place in the Spotlight or Behind the Curtain

Auditions, Performing & Stage Crew

No prior experience is required—our volunteer company welcomes newcomers and seasoned theatre veterans alike.

Auditions & Performing

From full-scale musicals and dramatic plays to holiday variety shows and cabarets, we hold open community auditions throughout the year.

  • Open, welcoming casting for adults, teens, and youth
  • Opportunities for vocalists, actors, dancers, and instrumentalists
  • Rehearsal schedules designed around work and school hours

Backstage & Stage Crew

Every great show depends on the creative hands behind the scenes. Join our technical and production crew to help bring our productions to life.

  • Set design, carpentry, scenic painting & stagecraft
  • Lighting & sound board operation
  • Costumes, props, stage management & front-of-house box office

Youth Theatre & SWAG

Our youth outreach—including the SWAG (Students With Amazing Gifts) program—empowers young performers and student technicians across Bradford County.

  • Youth workshops in acting, improv, and stage confidence
  • Junior Board Liaison leadership opportunities
  • Mentorship from local educators and theatre artists
Invest in Local Arts

Sponsors & Donors

As a 100% volunteer-driven 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization, every dollar from ticket sales, sponsorships, and tax-deductible donations goes directly into production royalties, scripts, youth programming, and preserving our historic downtown home venue.

Partner With Winding River Players

Local businesses and individual patrons keep live theatre thriving in Bradford County. Sponsors receive recognition in our printed playbills, lobby displays at the Black Box Theatre, and shout-outs across our website and social channels.

Our Home Venue in Downtown Towanda

The Black Box Theatre

While some special productions take place at partner venues around the community, our home stage is located in the heart of historic downtown Towanda.

Winding River Players at The Black Box Theatre 415 Main Street Towanda, PA 18848 Email: contact@windingriverplayers.org
